Cellulose polymers (CA)

Overview & uses

Films, moulded products and selected fibres.

Family-level source ranges, not a declared construction grade. * = source estimate. Source prices are historical references. Original PDF includes the supporting notes and photographs.

Composition

(C5H4O[CH2OR-(OR2)]-O-)n R = COCH3 or H. Typical DS (degree of acetyl substitution) is 2.5. Commonly plasticized with phthalate esters such as dimethyl phthalate, diethyl phthalate, also with triphenyl phosphate or triacetin.
