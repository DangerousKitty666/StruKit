Composition

3.5-4.0:1.5:1:6:1:0.1:1:cement (ASTM Type 2):silica fume (microsilica):ground quartz:silica sand:steel fiber:superplasticizer:water (Note: no course aggregate) Mix constituents and proportions are varied to produce widely ranging properties.
