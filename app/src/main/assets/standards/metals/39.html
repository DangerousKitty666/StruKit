Asphalt concrete

Overview & uses

Road surfacing and paved areas.

Family-level source ranges, not a declared construction grade. * = source estimate. Source prices are historical references. Original PDF includes the supporting notes and photographs.

Composition

A mix of sand and aggregate with bitumen (asphalt). Typical bitumen content: 4-10% by weight. Rubbery polymers are sometimes added to the mix to improve properties.
