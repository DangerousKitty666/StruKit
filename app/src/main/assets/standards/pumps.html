Pumps · duty, power & suction

Equations, worked examples & charts. Expand a subject; original references are linked inside.

1 · Head and pressure
Δp = ρgH
H = Δp/(ρg)

H in metres of the pumped liquid. Pressure depends on density even when head is unchanged.

Worked example

10 m head at ρ = 1000 kg/m³ equals 98.07 kPa = 0.981 bar.

2 · Hydraulic, shaft and electrical power
Pₕ = ρgQH
Pshaft = Pₕ/ηpump
Pelec = Pshaft/ηmotor

Q in m³/s and power in W. Efficiencies are fractions and must correspond to the operating point.

Worked example

Q = 0.01, H = 20 m, ρ = 1000 gives 1.961 kW hydraulic; at ηpump = 0.7 and ηmotor = 0.9, input is 3.113 kW.

3 · System energy balance
Hp = Δz + Δp/(ρg) + Δ(v²)/(2g) + hL

Use a consistent inlet/outlet datum. Static elevation, pressure difference, velocity change and losses all contribute.

Worked example

An open-tank lift of 8 m plus 4 m loss needs 12 m head if terminal velocities are negligible.

4 · Pipe and local losses
hf = f(L/D)v²/(2g)
hminor = ΣK v²/(2g)

f is the Darcy friction factor, not Fanning. L and D in m. Determine f from Reynolds number and relative roughness; use fitting K for the correct geometry.

Worked example

f = 0.02, L = 20 m, D = 0.05 m and v = 2 m/s gives hf = 1.63 m.

5 · Speed affinity laws
Q₂/Q₁ = n₂/n₁
H₂/H₁ = (n₂/n₁)²
P₂/P₁ = (n₂/n₁)³

Same pump geometry, liquid and approximately constant efficiency; evaluate actual duty against the system curve. Do not apply blindly to major impeller trimming.

Worked example

At 80% speed, ideal flow/head/power ratios are 0.8 / 0.64 / 0.512.

6 · Operating point
Hpump(Q) = Hsystem(Q)

Intersection of pump and system curves determines steady flow. The example curves below are invented for explanation, not a pump selection chart.

Worked example

Hpump = 30 − 0.02q² and Hsystem = 5 + 0.03q², with q in L/s, intersect at q = 22.36 L/s, H = 20 m.

7 · Suction head and cavitation
NPSHa = (pabs − pv)/(ρg) + zs − hfs

Open-reservoir form referenced to pump datum: zs positive when liquid surface is above the pump; hfs is suction loss. Compare with manufacturer NPSHr plus the required margin.

Worked example

pabs = 101.325 kPa, pv = 2.34 kPa, ρ = 1000, zs = 1 m, hfs = 0.5 m gives NPSHa ≈ 10.59 m.

8 · Series and parallel pumps
Series: Htotal(Q) = H₁(Q)+H₂(Q)
Parallel: Qtotal(H) = Q₁(H)+Q₂(H)

Add head at equal flow for series; flow at equal head for parallel. The new operating point still depends on the system.

Worked example

Two pumps each supplying 10 L/s at 15 m give a parallel curve point of 20 L/s at 15 m, not necessarily the final duty.
