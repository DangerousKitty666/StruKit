Sheet size and actual weight

Multiply kg/m² by length × width in metres for the complete panel. Example: an 18 mm birch panel at 680 kg/m³ has 12.24 kg/m²; a 2.44 × 1.22 m sheet is approximately 36.44 kg. Moisture, coatings and manufacturing tolerances change actual weight.
