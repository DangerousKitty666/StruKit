Standards overview

Numbers below organise this app’s reference notes; they are not clauses of a standard. Always identify the complete designation, part, edition and applicable National Annex.

1 · ISO / EN ISO

International / European

ISO is the international designation. EN ISO identifies European adoption. Always match the part number and edition.

Reference ↗

2 · BS EN / BS EN ISO

United Kingdom

British adoption of an EN or EN ISO standard. BS EN 15048 and BS EN 14399 cover structural bolting assemblies.

Reference ↗

3 · ČSN EN / ČSN EN ISO

Czech Republic

Czech adoption of European standards. ČSN EN ISO 4014 refers to the adopted hexagon-head bolt standard; verify the edition.

Reference ↗

4 · DIN / DIN EN ISO

Germany

DIN-only numbers are often encountered in older drawings and catalogues. DIN EN ISO is the German adoption of EN ISO. A familiar replacement number does not prove dimensional interchangeability.

Reference ↗

5 · NF EN · UNI EN · UNE EN · LST EN

Other European adoptions

French, Italian, Spanish and Lithuanian prefixes identify national adoptions. Match the full EN/ISO number, part and edition rather than the prefix alone.

Reference ↗

6 · GOST / GOST ISO / GOST R

Russia and interstate specifications

GOST 7798-70 is a hexagon-head bolt reference encountered in drawings. GOST, GOST ISO and GOST R are distinct designations. Do not substitute an ISO or DIN part solely by visual similarity.

Reference ↗

7 · GOST 5915-70 / GOST 5916-70

Hexagon nuts / thin hexagon nuts

These are accuracy-class B nut references. Thread, dimensions and mechanical requirements must be checked against the specified GOST edition rather than assumed from an ISO nut table.

Reference ↗

8 · GOST 11371-78

Normal-series washers

Washer specification covering accuracy classes A and C. Check the selected size and class rather than assuming DIN 125 or ISO 7089 dimensions.

Reference ↗

9 · ISO 4014 · historical DIN 931

Partially threaded hex bolts

Reference b depends on diameter and length range. Head widths at some diameters differ from older DIN dimensions.

Reference ↗

10 · ISO 4017 · historical DIN 933

Hex screws, thread to head

Under-head length L includes the threaded shank. Incomplete thread and under-head runout are not full load-bearing thread.

Reference ↗

11 · ISO 4762 · historical DIN 912

Socket head cap screws

s identifies the internal hexagon, not the head outside diameter. k is the cylindrical head height.

Reference ↗

12 · ISO 7380-1 / ISO 7380-2

Button-head socket screws

Part 1 covers the button-head form; part 2 includes a collar. Reduced head loadability must be considered; these are not interchangeable with structural hex bolt assemblies.

Reference ↗

13 · ISO 4032 / ISO 4035

Hexagon nuts / thin nuts

Nut style and property class matter. A thin nut must not be assigned the load capacity of a full-height nut.

Reference ↗

14 · ISO 10511 · historical DIN 985

Thin prevailing-torque nuts with insert

A common reference family for nylon-insert locknuts. Confirm the applicable edition, height, thread range and prevailing-torque requirements; the historical DIN reference is not an unrestricted equivalence claim.

Reference ↗

15 · ISO 7089 / ISO 7093-1

Normal / large-series plain washers

Outside diameter, thickness, hardness and product grade depend on the specified washer standard.

Reference ↗

16 · EN 15048

Non-preloaded structural bolting

An assembly specification, not merely a bolt shape. Use the specified compatible bolt/nut assembly and documentation.

Reference ↗

17 · EN 14399 series

High-strength assemblies for preloading

The series specifies matched assemblies and their suitability for preloading. Do not mix components based only on diameter and strength markings.
