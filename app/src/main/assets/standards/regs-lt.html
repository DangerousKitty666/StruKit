1 · Purpose and complexity are different

Identify the use/purpose (paskirtis) and the complexity category (kategorija) separately. Buildings and engineering structures are classified under STR 1.01.03:2017 “Statinių ir patalpų klasifikavimas”. Buildings include residential (gyvenamieji) and non-residential (negyvenamieji) uses; premises can have their own classification.

2 · Nesudėtingieji — simple structures

Small, simple structures may fall in this category, divided into groups I and II. Examples can include qualifying ancillary buildings, greenhouses or engineering structures. The January 2026 VTPSI guide notes that some qualifying residential buildings up to 50 m² and 5 m height may be included; these are conditional criteria, not a general exemption. A small footprint alone does not establish the category; check use, dimensions and construction against the current STR tables. Permit/start-notification requirements also depend on location and the work.

3 · Neypatingieji — non-special structures

Structures outside both the simple and special categories. Typical individual houses may fall here. Confirm the actual parameters rather than assuming a category from the name “house”. Category affects the design, competence and approval requirements.

4 · Ypatingieji — special structures

Includes qualifying complex structures/technologies, hazardous substances or equipment, public-use buildings with more than 100 simultaneous occupants and apartment buildings with more than five storeys, and cultural-heritage structures. Classification triggers additional competence and project-control considerations. Consult the statutory criteria for the exact building and use.

5 · Permits and commencement

Check the Statybos įstatymas and the current permit/commencement rules for new construction, reconstruction, repair, demolition or change of use. A simple structure is not automatically exempt: protected areas, heritage, urban location and the type of work can change requirements. Record whether an SLD (statybą leidžiantis dokumentas) and commencement notification are required before starting.

7 · Official documents and current editions

Start with the Construction Law (Statybos įstatymas), STR 1.01.03:2017 (classification), STR 1.04.04:2017 (design/expertise), STR 1.05.01:2017 (permits/completion and related procedures) and the relevant technical/fire requirements. Select the consolidation effective on the project’s governing date. The register also exposes a future 1 January 2027 classification edition; do not apply it early.
