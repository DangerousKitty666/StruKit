Dynamics · motion & vibration

Equations, worked examples & charts. Expand a subject; original references are linked inside.

1 · Constant acceleration
v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as

Use m, s and m/s²; u is initial velocity. All equations assume constant acceleration along one axis.

Worked example

u = 2 m/s, a = 1.5 m/s², t = 4 s gives v = 8 m/s and s = 20 m.

2 · Force and weight
ΣF = ma
W = mg

Net force and weight in N; mass in kg. Include all forces with a consistent sign convention. Standard g = 9.80665 m/s²; local gravity varies.

Worked example

A 250 kg load weighs about 2452 N. Accelerating it upward at 0.5 m/s² requires about 2577 N before friction.

3 · Work and energy
W = F s cos θ
Eₖ = ½mv²
Eₚ = mgh

Work and energy in J. θ is the angle between constant force and displacement. Potential-energy datum may be chosen freely.

Worked example

A 40 kg trolley moving at 3 m/s has 180 J of kinetic energy.

4 · Power, torque and speed
ω = 2πn/60
P = Tω

n in rpm; ω in rad/s; torque T in N·m; shaft power P in W. Electrical input differs because of losses.

Worked example

A 15 N·m shaft at 1200 rpm transmits 1885 W.

5 · Circular motion
v = ωr
aᶜ = v²/r = ω²r
Fᶜ = maᶜ

r in m. Centripetal acceleration points towards the centre. Tangential acceleration is a separate component.

Worked example

A 2 kg body at 4 m/s on a 0.5 m radius requires 64 N inward force.

6 · Impulse and momentum
J = ∫F dt = Δ(mv)
Favg = Δp/Δt

Impulse J in N·s; momentum p in kg·m/s. Average impact force does not give the peak force.

Worked example

Stopping a 10 kg mass from 2 m/s over 0.1 s requires 200 N average net force.

7 · Rotational inertia
T = Iα
Eᵣ = ½Iω²

I in kg·m²; α in rad/s². Mass moment of inertia is not the area second moment used for beam bending.

Worked example

A solid 10 kg disc of radius 0.2 m has I = 0.2 kg·m²; at 20 rad/s it stores 40 J.

8 · Spring–mass vibration
ωₙ = √(k/m)
fₙ = ωₙ/(2π)
Tperiod = 1/fₙ

Undamped linear single-degree-of-freedom system. k in N/m; m in kg. Real joints, damping and distributed mass alter the result.

Worked example

k = 20000 N/m and m = 50 kg give fₙ = 3.18 Hz and period 0.314 s.
