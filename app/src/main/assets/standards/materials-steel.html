Carbon-steel thermal basis

EN 1993-1-2 carbon-steel expressions, evaluated at θ = 20°C: λ = 54 − 0.0333θ; cₚ = 425 + 0.773θ − 0.00169θ² + 0.00000222θ³. These are engineering models rather than grade-specific certificates. Diffusivity = λ/(ρcₚ). Do not extrapolate these polynomial branches beyond their stated temperature ranges.
