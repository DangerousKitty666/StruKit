1 · Density, mass and weight
ρ = m/V
m = ρV
W = mg

ρ in kg/m³; V in m³; W in N. Density is a material property; mass depends on the part volume.

Worked example

A 0.02 m³ steel part at 7850 kg/m³ has mass 157 kg and weight approximately 1540 N.

2 · Axial stiffness and strain
σ = F/A
ε = ΔL/L
σ = Eε
ΔL = FL/(EA)

Uniform elastic bar, small strain. Use N and mm with E in N/mm² to obtain ΔL in mm.

Worked example

F = 10000 N, L = 1000 mm, A = 100 mm², E = 210000 N/mm² gives ΔL = 0.476 mm.

3 · Elastic constants
G = E/[2(1+ν)]
K = E/[3(1−2ν)]

Isotropic linear elastic material. G is shear modulus; K is bulk modulus. This shortcut is not valid for timber across and along grain.

Worked example

E = 210 GPa and ν = 0.30 give G = 80.77 GPa and K = 175 GPa.

4 · Free thermal expansion
ΔL = αLΔT

α in 1/K, L and ΔL in the same length unit. Temperature-dependent expansion coefficients may be mean values across a stated interval.

Worked example

A 6 m steel member, α = 12×10⁻⁶/K, heated by 40 K expands 2.88 mm.

5 · Thermal storage
Q = mcₚΔT
Cᵥ = ρcₚ

Heat Q in J; volumetric heat capacity Cᵥ in J/(m³·K). Account for temperature-dependent cₚ and phase changes when relevant.

Worked example

10 kg at cₚ = 500 J/(kg·K), heated by 20 K, stores 100 kJ.

6 · Heat diffusion
a = λ/(ρcₚ)

a in m²/s. A rough diffusion timescale across distance L is L²/a; boundary conditions determine the actual transient response.

Worked example

λ = 15 W/(m·K), ρ = 8000 and cₚ = 500 give a = 3.75×10⁻⁶ m²/s.

7 · Electrical resistance
R = ρₑ L/A

Resistivity ρₑ in Ω·m; length in m; area in m². Electrical resistance depends on both material and geometry.

Worked example

ρₑ = 0.75×10⁻⁶ Ω·m, L = 1 m and A = 10 mm² give R = 0.075 Ω.

8 · Choosing property data
grade + condition + temperature + test direction

Use mean density for estimated mass, declared strength for design and the relevant directional stiffness for deflection. Values for polymer stock shapes are not automatically valid for printed parts.

Worked example

For an aluminium bracket, “6082” alone does not define yield strength: specify temper, product and thickness.
