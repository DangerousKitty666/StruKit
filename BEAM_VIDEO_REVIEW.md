## How the app uses these references

21 conceptual arrangements are modelled independently, with a reverse view and preparation notes. Repeated videos are grouped by connection arrangement rather than treated as different connections simply because the title differs. The cover-plate splice and flush-end-plate entries retain conventional concepts from the existing library; the linked channel videos are related references, not claims of exact reproduction.

The review is based on thumbnails and selected preparation/assembly frames, not uninterrupted viewing of every video. Some clips provide only an overview or preparation frame. No unseen weld, concealed bolt, dimension, load rating or certified connection category is inferred from an obscured view. Gold highlights in the app identify selected weld locations, not a complete weld schedule. All geometry is illustrative, not a fabrication detail.
